Coco was the fiercely protective mother of Kaddu and the undisputed queen of her territory. Though a devoted parent, she often behaved like a child herself — playful, free-spirited, and constantly seeking attention from her favourite humans. She had mastered the art of communication, offering paw shakes without ever being taught. Call her from a distance and she would sprint toward you, launching herself into an enthusiastic hug. Fearless with unfamiliar dogs, Coco never hesitated to assert her authority. She is deeply missed.
