Patlu was cautious around new people. She liked to observe from a distance before deciding whether someone was worth trusting. If you tried to touch her too soon, she would squeal loudly, part protest, part performance, to let both you and her mother know she was not pleased. During vet visits, Patlu was often the hardest to catch for this very reason. Even with three people blocking every direction, she would sprint off as fast as her tiny legs could carry her, expertly dodging us each time.
